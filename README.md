## 👋 About Me

I'm **Aayush Singhavi**, an **Automation & Robotics Engineering student** interested in building intelligent systems that connect software, hardware and the physical world.

My work sits at the intersection of:

* 🤖 **Robotics & Autonomous Systems**
* 🧠 **Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning**
* 👁️ **Computer Vision**
* 🔌 **Embedded Systems & IoT**
* ⚙️ **Automation & Control Systems**
* 💻 **Full-Stack Software Engineering**
* 🛠️ **CAD / Product Design / 3D Printing**

I enjoy taking a real-world problem, breaking it down into systems, designing the architecture, building the prototype and then turning it into something that can actually work outside a notebook.

> **I don't just want to build projects.
> I want to build systems that solve problems.**
